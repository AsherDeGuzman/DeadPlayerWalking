1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Game title: Dead Player Walking

Engine: Godot Engine 4.7.2

Programming language: GDScript

Game type: 2D top-down action roguelike

Primary platform: Mobile

Additional development platform: Desktop for testing and debugging

Visual direction: Dark fantasy pixel art

Core gameplay concept:

The player descends through a dangerous dungeon consisting of 10 floors. Each floor contains interconnected combat rooms populated by enemies.

The player fights through these rooms using two unique weapons:
- Dungeon Edge — melee weapon.
- Warden Crossbow — ranged weapon.
Enemies must be defeated before locked room exits become accessible.

The central mechanic revolves around previous versions of the player. When the player is defeated, their character progression and combat capabilities contribute to the creation of a previous-self enemy that can appear in future encounters.

The player can spend earned currency after defeat to purchase permanent character and weapon upgrades.

The objective is to descend through all 10 floors and defeat the final boss on Floor 10.
